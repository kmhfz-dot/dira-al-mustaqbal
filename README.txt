DIRA AL MUSTAQBAL TRADING - FULL CATALOGUE WEBSITE
==================================================

This package contains 19 working full-PDF catalogue viewers and 18 catalogue entries waiting for their PDF files.

HOW TO OPEN LOCALLY
1. Extract the ZIP completely.
2. Open index.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
3. Scroll to Catalogue by Sector.
4. Click any item marked Open full catalogue.

HOW TO PUBLISH
Upload the entire dira-al-mustaqbal-v2-lite folder to your web host. Do not upload only index.html because the PDF viewers depend on the assets/brochures folder.
